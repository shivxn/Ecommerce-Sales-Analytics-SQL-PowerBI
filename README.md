# Ecommerce Sales Analytics - SQL + Tableau

![Dashboard](Dashboard 1.png)

## Business Problem
An online store had 10,000+ rows of sales data but didn't know:
- Which products are actually making profit?
- Which customers are most valuable?
- Why is sales going down in some months?

They needed clear answers, not just raw data.

## What I Did
- Cleaned 10,000+ rows of messy data using SQL
- Analyzed sales, profit & customer behaviour
- Built an interactive Tableau dashboard for monthly sales tracking

## Key Findings
- **Sales:** $4.29M YTD | **Profit:** $0.50M | **Orders:** 60,622
- **Top Category:** Phones & Copiers are driving maximum sales
- **Top Market:** USCA contributes 31.94% of total sales, followed by Asia Pacific
- **Repeat Customers:** Spend 3x more than new customers
- **Seasonality:** December sales are 40% higher than June — best time for offers

## Dashboard Includes
- YTD vs PYTD Sales, Profit & Order Quantity
- Category-wise YTD vs PYTD Sales
- Market-wise Customer Acquisition by Month
- Market Share by Sales
